How It Works

Three steps.
No friction.

LexiFlow lives in your browser and activates exactly when you need it — nothing more.

1

Select text

Highlight any word, sentence, or paragraph on any webpage.

2

Press shortcut

Hit Alt+L to trigger LexiFlow instantly.

3

Understand instantly

Translation, pronunciation, and explanation appear inline — no tab switch.
